Fix: a label that is yes/no shaped, has "you / I / the applicant" as subject and a status phrase (`status_phrases`) as its head noun, and matches no shape, becomes a G3 status question (`plain=False`, the facts line) rather than None; add `have you (ever )?been`, `will you be`, `holder`, `as of`, `since birth`, `dual citizenship` / `citizenship of another country` (kind "citizen", country unknown, G3) to `_NC_SHAPES`; keep the (a) group below out (PR manager, citizen scientist).
